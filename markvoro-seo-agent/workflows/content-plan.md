# Content Plan

Produces a prioritized content calendar — new posts/pages and existing-page
refreshes — rather than editing anything.

## Steps

1. [keyword-research](keyword-research.md) — fresh clusters and gaps.
2. [competitor-analysis](../skills/competitor-analysis/SKILL.md) — where
   competitors cover a topic MARKVORO doesn't (skip with a note if
   `../config/competitors.md` is empty).
3. [content-seo](../skills/content-seo/SKILL.md) — brief each planned
   piece: cluster, intent, MARKVORO-specific angle, outline, internal
   links to include. Also flag existing content worth refreshing instead
   of writing something new that would cannibalize it.
4. Prioritize the resulting list by business relevance (does it map to a
   real MARKVORO service — see `../config/brand.md`) and by the Priority
   levels in `../config/seo-rules.md`, not by volume alone (no fabricated
   volume numbers per `../config/guardrails.md`).
5. Write `../data/keywords/<date>-content-briefs.md`.

## Output

A content calendar: piece title/topic, target cluster, intent, priority,
new vs. refresh, brief. No content is written or published by this
workflow — that's a separate step once a brief is approved.
